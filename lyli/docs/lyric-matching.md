# Lyric matching

Scoring version 22 retains automatic selection and the user's source-priority mode. It assumes music providers usually return usable lyrics and improves comparisons between those candidates rather than requiring manual confirmation for ordinary playback.

## Cross-platform catalog aliases

Version 20 shares an artist-scoped catalog alias between provider prefilters, ranking and official-word fusion. Mintone's Chinese catalog lists `飞` by `Matt吕彦良`; the international release lists `Fly` by `Matt Lv`. The entry recognizes `吕彦良`, `Matt吕彦良`, `Matt Lv` and their spacing/script variants, plus the bilingual Fly/飞 title. This is an explicit catalog entry, not a general translation rule. Other artists' Fly tracks remain unrelated to 飞.

External providers first search the canonical catalog name and artist, then retry the original metadata and international Fly / Matt Lv metadata after a miss or failure (at most three distinct queries). A matching timed recording ends the retry; plain text, instrumental markers and recording conflicts do not. Other artists retain their existing search path, and Apple Music's local lookup always receives the original metadata. Recording qualifiers are preserved. Unrelated titles such as the previously cached Flying (Live) are rejected for this known track, even if an artist credit overlaps. Duration, lyric validity and fusion text/clock checks still apply. Existing caches are not globally replaced; explicit re-matching runs the new rule. Rejected candidates display the rejection reason instead of a misleading -10000 score.

## Apple cache association when catalog entries are missing

Music can retain a successful TTML response after evicting its corresponding catalog song record. `read` remains a pure read-only cache operation. `search` first tries that local association, then resolves only song IDs already present in local lyric responses through Apple's unsigned public iTunes lookup API if no safe local match exists. The request uses an ephemeral session without cookies or credentials; only metadata is fetched. It is bounded to 160 IDs, an eight-second timeout and a two-megabyte response. A shared in-memory lookup avoids repeated successful requests and applies a 120-second cooldown to unresolved IDs. Failure preserves the existing fallback behavior.

Supplemental metadata must pass the same song ID, title, artist, album and duration checks before the existing local TTML is accepted. Traditional/Simplified catalog text is normalized for matching; titles also normalize 妳/你. Recording labels remain part of the title and normal version checks. A malformed newer TTML response does not suppress an older complete response for the same song ID. No writes are made to Music's cache database or response files.

The reported 明明他已离开你 failure was an orphaned lyric response: the public catalog ID 1446374585 resolved to 张敬轩 / Senses Inherited / 289.213 seconds, while the local cache retained 44 timed lyric rows without its catalog record. Real cache contents stay outside the repository.

## Evidence used for ranking

Provider-reported track duration is the primary duration signal. A difference within two seconds or one percent earns the full 300 points; larger differences reduce that bonus, and a difference above twelve percent retains the existing 250-point penalty. Missing or zero duration is unknown, not a mismatch. When reported duration is unavailable, the last useful lyric timestamp supplies a weaker bonus of at most 100 points. Lyrics extending more than five seconds past the track retain their existing penalties. Blank timestamps and lyric credits do not supply an endpoint or contribute to the line-count bonus. An instrumental outro does not reduce the duration bonus when the reported track duration matches.

Word timing must parse into at least two useful lines with distinct line timestamps and nondecreasing word starts within each line. Invalid or fragmentary tracks fall back to the candidate's LRC before ranking, preview and persistence. Valid word timing retains its 400-point preference. This validation establishes basic usability, not alignment to the audio; playback still applies its existing timeline normalization.

Content agreement counts each other provider once. Comparisons use normalized adjacent character triples so punctuation and capitalization differences are tolerated across English and Chinese while a shared bag of words is insufficient. Both the candidate and its supporting peers must have matching song/artist information and no identified recording-version conflict; an explicitly wrong recording cannot borrow the requested recording's text-agreement bonus. Content agreement does not validate timestamps or establish that providers obtained their lyrics independently.

Recording-version comparison recognizes performance differences such as live, acoustic, karaoke and demo in titles and album metadata, including attached Chinese labels. Album detection of English live recordings uses explicit forms such as `Live at`, `Live in` and `(Live)` rather than any appearance of the word. A candidate without requested version information receives a smaller penalty than an explicit version mismatch. Remaster labels, including a preceding year, do not incur a recording-version penalty. If another usable candidate matches the title or recording version better, a conflicting candidate cannot use its word-timing bonus to overcome that difference. A sole usable candidate with incomplete version metadata remains selectable.

Only fully identical candidates are deduplicated. Different albums, timestamps, translations or word tracks remain available for comparison. Equal scores within a source use a deterministic tie-break so response order does not decide the winner.

The search result carries the specific winning candidate through automatic saving, re-matching and default search-window selection. A provider name identifies a source, not one candidate: a source can return several recordings or an instrumental marker before a timed lyric. Saved lyrics, translation, word timing and score must all come from the selected candidate. Searches with only plain text or instrumental results continue through their existing fallback handling. Candidate persistence continues to carry the exact selected match.

## Validation and limits

The matching selftest covers repeated-provider votes, independent votes, reordered words, English and Chinese punctuation, invalid/partial/decreasing word timing, long outros, blank and credit endpoints, unknown durations, distinct same-source variants, deterministic ties, karaoke/live/acoustic conflicts, remaster labels and source-priority selection. Workflow tests verify fallback persistence, scoring-version metadata and saving the better same-source variant alongside the existing search, editing and playback regressions.

A read-only compatibility check of the local 123-entry cache found 121 timed lyrics and 89 word tracks; all 89 word tracks passed the new basic validation. Personal lyrics and audit outputs are not included in the repository. This check confirms compatibility for that cache, not a measured matching-accuracy improvement.

Apple Music cache hits can upgrade existing automatic lyrics when a song starts playing or the app launches. Manual picks (including unlocked picks), edits, explicit source choices, pinned songs and calibrated lyrics are protected. A cache miss preserves existing lyrics. Other existing cache records are not re-scored on launch. New searches record version 22; explicit re-matching evaluates the new rules while retaining the existing protections for manual edits and calibrated lyrics. The score remains a heuristic ordering rather than a probability of correctness. This change introduces neither a universal score threshold nor an audio-based synchronization check.


## Official-clock word fusion (version 3)

`LyricsFusion` keeps Apple's original text, translation, line starts and clear markers authoritative. Existing native Apple word timing is preserved. External priors are explicit in `externalSourceWeights`, defaulting to equal weights because provider-specific accuracy has not been measured; callers can supply bounded positive weights. Weights cannot bypass song identity, duration, embedded offset, monotone anchors, whole-song clock consistency or line-boundary checks.

Fusion uses the same title/album recording-version classifier as ranking and requires equal recording-version sets, including when an official version is known but a donor omits it. Each donor receives one median whole-song offset correction. Local differences remain in the word starts; individual rows are no longer independently snapped to official starts. The original whole-song offsets must also agree within 250 ms before sources can support each other, so correction cannot erase a clock conflict. Official line visibility and clear boundaries remain authoritative; words exceeding the boundary tolerance fall back per line. A shared systematic error relative to the audio still cannot be diagnosed without audio evidence.

Candidate rows align to the official text over at most three adjacent source rows and only at existing word boundaries. Traditional/Simplified forms, 妳/你, punctuation and zero-duration spacing are normalized for comparison, then the original official text is restored. Crossed 2:2 line breaks work without interpolating characters inside a source chunk. Remaining real-word differences fall back per line.

Aligned candidates contribute at most one vote per provider per official line. Same-provider variants select the trajectory supported by other providers, with deterministic ties. With at most five external providers, the consensus enumerates mutually agreeing groups: shared boundary starts must stay within 250 ms, their mean difference within 150 ms, and line-end difference within 250 ms. A unique group of at least two providers must exceed half the participating weight. Equal groups, a split vote or an isolated high-weight outlier cannot create consensus. A sole validated source remains usable, as in version 1. Different validated sources may fill different lines.

Within the accepted group, observed matching chunk starts and ends use weighted medians. The finest observed segmentation with its own strict majority supporting every boundary is retained. A coarse third source does not downgrade a finer trajectory supported by two agreeing sources; only those two sources count as supporting that fine trajectory. If a coarse source supports only phrase boundaries, finer internal word boundaries are not promoted as consensus. Chunk starts and ends must be observed in each participating source, and corresponding chunk ends must agree within 250 ms. Resulting word order, durations, official text, line boundaries and at least 55% coverage of both useful lyric lines and normalized lyric characters remain required. Credit lines do not affect coverage. Multi-source metadata records every contributing provider, plus multi-source/single-source line coverage, while version-1 and version-2 provenance remain readable. The lyrics manager displays these counts without claiming audio verification. This is a consistency check, not proof that the audio is synchronized or that providers are independent.

Automatic enrichment runs in a utility task after official lyrics are visible. Old automatic fusion versions upgrade on playback; if an upgrade cannot produce a safe result, official line lyrics replace the obsolete word track and later retries retain the cooldown. Genuine native Apple word tracks and manual/calibrated records stay protected. Failed attempts use a 120-second cooldown instead of blocking retries for the entire session. Late results must still match the cache revision, current track and enabled providers before saving.
